Fake Meet: how to use

A real Meet page, captured, for driving the real extension by hand. The Bots button and its panel come from the installed content script; this page only plays the part of Meet.

Build with pnpm build:dev, load build/chrome-mv3-dev unpacked, serve with pnpm play (or open from disk with "Allow access to file URLs" ticked), then reload this tab.

Scenarios: host | ?bots=20 crowded | ?guest=1 not the host | ?bare=1 late header. They combine: ?guest=1&bots=20.

In use: no script
